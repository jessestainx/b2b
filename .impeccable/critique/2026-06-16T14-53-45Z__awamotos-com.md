#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: This doesn't look AI-generated; rather, it exhibits the classic symptoms of a rigid e-commerce template. The primary anti-pattern is **Identical card grids**. The page repeats the exact same 5-column product card layout four times consecutively. This creates a "Wall of Options" cognitive overload.

**Deterministic scan**: No CLI scan was run since this is a live URL.

#### Overall Impression
A solid, functional B2B e-commerce foundation that feels a bit sterile and repetitive. The B2B logged-out state communicates clearly, but the overall layout lacks rhythm and premium polish.

#### What's Working
1. **B2B Context**: The banner clearly explaining that prices require an approved CNPJ prevents user frustration.
2. **Clear Navigation**: The top header and categories are distinct and easy to find.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Identical Grid Fatigue**:
  - **What**: Four consecutive product shelves use the exact same layout.
  - **Why it matters**: It destroys visual rhythm. Users stop scanning and start glossing over the content because it all looks the same.
  - **Fix**: Introduce layout variety. Make "Lançamentos" a featured grid, or alter the card density between sections.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P2] Extraneous Cognitive Load (Logged-out state)**:
  - **What**: Every single product card displays an identical "VER PREÇOS" button that just acts as a login prompt.
  - **Why it matters**: Since the user can't buy anyway, showing 20 identical CTAs on screen creates visual noise without adding value.
  - **Fix**: Tone down the logged-out state. Mute the CTA or rely on the global B2B banner, emphasizing the product details instead of a blocked action.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

- **[P2] Sterile Information Blocks**:
  - **What**: The four benefit cards (Preços de atacado, etc.) and the B2B warning box feel flat and boxy.
  - **Why it matters**: These are key trust and conversion elements for a B2B store. They should feel premium and reassuring, not like structural placeholders.
  - **Fix**: Add subtle micro-interactions, refine the borders, and use a more deliberate color strategy.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable bolder`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Faces a "Wall of Options" due to the identical carousels. The constant repetition of "VER PREÇOS" might feel aggressively gated, though the explanation helps.

**Alex (Power User)**: The repetitive layout makes scanning for specific categories or deals difficult because every shelf carries the exact same visual weight.

#### Minor Observations
- The "NOVO" badge is very heavy and dark; it draws the eye away from the product images.
- The top header has a lot of thin grey lines that could be cleaned up.

#### Questions to Consider
- Does every product shelf need to be a carousel, or could some be masonry or featured grids?
- What would a more premium, trustworthy B2B warning banner look like?
